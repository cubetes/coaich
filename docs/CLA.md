## Why there is a CLA

CoAIch is AGPL-3.0, and that stays true for everyone. But the AGPL can't ride along into Apple's App Store:

- The App Store adds its own terms to every app (Apple's licence for users, limits on devices and copying). The
  AGPL forbids adding any further restrictions on the people who receive the software (section 10). The Free
  Software Foundation's position is that the two conflict, and GPL-licensed apps such as VLC have been pulled from
  the App Store over exactly this.
- Only the people who hold the copyright in the code can release it under other terms. Without a CLA, every
  contributor holds the copyright in their own lines, so the iPhone and Watch apps could not include a single
  outside contribution unless each contributor gave permission separately.
- The CLA gives the maintainer that permission once (clause 2). That is what lets the official CoAIch apps ship on
  the App Store, while the same code stays AGPL-3.0 for everyone else: you can still run, study, change, self-host
  and share it.
