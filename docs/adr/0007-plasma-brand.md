# Plasma fuchsia is the brand

Owner decision, 2026-10-12. The brand is **Plasma fuchsia** (`#F05AD8` on ink `#0A0A0B`, `#A61E93` on bone
`#F4F1EA`), with Archivo for type, as specified in [docs/brand.md](../brand.md). It replaces the iPhone app's MAGMA rose
(`#F5184E` / `#FF3D6E`), which sat too close to the fail red and to Apple's Move ring.

Consequences:
- The repository, its diagrams and images use it now.
- The iPhone and Watch apps switch their default accent to Plasma in the design phase, after the 2026-11-23 Hevy
  switch ("function before polish" until then).
- The logo is the **Cut C** (owner's pick 2026-10-12, from eight concepts and a third round of three finalists).
  It is the capital C of the **CoAIch** wordmark, whose AI carries the logo's cut. The app icon switches to the
  Cut C in the design phase.
