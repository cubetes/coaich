# AI text is generated on the iPhone with Apple's on-device model

Apple's Workout Buddy is not available to third-party apps, so the buddy is ours. Its lines are written by Apple Foundation Models on the iPhone (on-device, offline, free; the phone is always in the athlete's pocket) and spoken through AirPods, with a Watch note as fallback. Running the model on the Watch needs Private Cloud Compute and an entitlement that a free developer account may not get, so it is deferred.
