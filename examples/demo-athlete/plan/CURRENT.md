## Program — Upper/lower, 4 days × 60 min (ul4i·60)

In force from 2030-01-07 · week 1 · level intermediate · goal both · file `plan/program.json`.

### Why this program

- We've put you in Intermediate: over 2 years and training now; at week 6 we offer you Advanced.
- Upper/lower, 4 days: you asked for 4 days; every muscle group is trained in at least 2 sessions a week (strength, and shorter sessions).
- The 60-minute version: every session fits in your 60 minutes (longest ≈ 57.5 min: 8 min warm-up, about 3¼ min per compound set, 2 min per isolation or paired set).
- Goal both: the main lift of each day runs 6–10 reps; other compounds 8–12, isolations 10–20. An easy week every 7th week (fewer sets, lighter, never a week off).
- Spine-loading lifts (squats, deadlifts, standing presses, barbell rows) always stop 2 or more reps short of failure.

### Notes

- At week 6 we offer you Advanced.
