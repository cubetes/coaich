# Volume landmarks (coaching bands)

Weekly sets per muscle, as **effective sets**: direct sets ×1 + each set that lists the muscle as secondary ×0.5
(Pelland 2025, SR/MA: the fractional count fit best). "Shoulders" in the catalogue is split by name: rear delts =
rear delt, reverse fly, face pull; side delts = lateral raise, upright row; front delts = everything else.
"Middle back" counts as upper back.

## What is evidence and what is not

- **Evidence floors.** ≥ 4 effective sets a week per muscle group: the lowest bin that still grew (Schoenfeld,
  Ogborn & Krieger 2017, SR/MA). ≥ 10 a week: the hypertrophy optimum (Currier 2026, SR/MA). The audit (G1)
  uses these; at 30/45-minute sessions it uses 3 and 6 (our rule) and the plan says shorter sessions grow muscle
  more slowly.
- **Coaching bands.** MEV, MAV and MRV below are RP coaching practice (Israetel), grade P: no study has measured
  them as cut-offs. Say "below the usual coaching band", never "below the minimum effective volume". Only MRV is
  a gate (G2), applied per group at the group's largest head (back = lats + upper back, delts = three heads), and
  per muscle for glutes, traps and abs.

"Cut: keep" is the band for non-priority muscles in a fat-loss phase, where the aim is to keep muscle: volume over
it spends recovery on muscle you are only maintaining. Priority muscles may still aim higher.

## Reading the numbers

- Indirect-heavy muscles (front delts, triceps, biceps, glutes, upper back) can look low on direct sets and be
  well covered on effective sets. Report both before calling a muscle under-trained.
- A muscle near MRV with stalled lifts and poor recovery signals is the case for fewer sets; one signal alone is
  not.
- Average over full training weeks; leave easy weeks and the current partial week out.
