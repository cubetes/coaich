# Evidence

The studies behind the training rules CoAIch ships. This file covers training only. It does not cover nutrition, posture or wearables. Every entry was checked against PubMed (authors, year, journal, PMID/DOI).

**Grades.** **SR/MA** = systematic review or meta-analysis (an overview of reviews counts here). **RCT** = randomised trial (within-subject designs included). **OBS** = survey or observational data. **Acute EMG** = muscle activity in one session, with no growth outcome. **P** = practitioner judgement, consensus or our own rule (Delphi panels and position stands count as P for gates).

**Rule.** A gate in the program audit needs SR/MA or RCT evidence behind its *direction*. A number that has only P behind it is labelled "our rule" (see the last section). Claims are kept as narrow as the study allows.

Years are the print-issue year unless noted. Where the online-first year differs, both are given.

## Our rules (practitioner)

These numbers are ours. Each one is labelled "our rule" wherever it appears. The direction may have support from the tables above, but the exact number does not.

- **Set floors at short sessions:** effective sets/wk of ≥ 4 for beginners, dropping to **3** at 30/45-minute sessions. Intermediate/advanced: **10** for chest, back, quads and hamstrings, dropping to **6** at 30/45 minutes. The 4 and 10 come from the studies above; **3 and 6 are our rule**.
- **MRV caps:** taken from RP coaching bands. Our rule.
- **Easy-week interval:** every **7th** week for intermediates and every **6th** for advanced, counted in sessions done, not calendar weeks. Beginners: triggered only. Our rule.
- **Beginner day cap:** at most **4** training days/wk for the first 12 weeks. Our rule.
- **Beginner set ramp:** **2** sets per exercise in weeks 1–4, then 3 from week 5. Our rule.
- **60+ rules:** **2** sets per exercise and **3** reps in reserve on compounds for the first **8** weeks. No 4–6 rep bracket. Our rule.
- **Spine-lift effort floor:** squat, hinge and other spine-loading lifts never go below **2** reps in reserve. Our rule.
- **Time formula:** **8 min** warm-up + **3.25 min** per compound set + **2 min** per isolation or paired set. Every session must fit inside the minutes the user answered. Our rule.
- **Placement thresholds:** training history (never/< 6 mo · 6 mo–2 y · > 2 y) × last regular training (≤ 3 mo · 3–12 mo · > 12 mo). A gap of > 3 months marks the user as returning. Promotion needs ≥ 12 weeks plus adherence and stall criteria. Our rule.
- **Known-miss list:** programs that miss a gate are listed in the audit test, each with a written reason. Any new miss fails the build. The list is our judgement, not evidence.
